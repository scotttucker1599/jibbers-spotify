# Spotify for Jibbers

A [Jibbers mod loader](https://github.com/jaxoncarelos/JibbersModLoader) C# mod: see and control what's playing in Spotify from the game, with a spinning record in the corner and a mini Spotify app on the phone. No Spotify login. The mod is in [Spotify](Spotify) (see its [README](Spotify/README.md)); its tests are in [Spotify.Tests](Spotify.Tests). Licensed GPL-3.0-only. Not made by or affiliated with Spotify.
